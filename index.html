Where the signals are pointing

Indicative only. The dashboard surfaces which scenarios the signals are most consistent with. It does not place a position on the 2×2 — that judgement stays with the room.
Slower adaptation  ←    Speed of industry adaptation    →  Faster adaptation
Contained  ←     Severity of disruption     →  Prolonged & severe
How this is read. Each scenario counts today's signals that are consistent with its conditions. Breached signals count 1.0; approaching signals count 0.5. Quiet signals count for nothing — a quiet market is not the same as a calming one, so only actively easing signals lift the low-severity scenarios. The dots show relative consistency, not a score out of five.

    About this prototype. Built to show the shape of the production dashboard. All figures are illustrative, calibrated to current market conditions (August 2026), not drawn from live feeds. In production, signals would refresh daily from named sources: ICE Brent and TTF, S&P Platts JKM, the WTO Strait of Hormuz Trade Tracker, the Caldara-Iacoviello Geopolitical Risk Index, the Drewry World Container Index, ASX 200 VIX, the NAB Monthly Business Survey, AEMO Quarterly Energy Dynamics, and ABS capex intentions. A consumer-goods tile is deliberately not shown; no clean single-index signal exists for the sector, and the team will decide whether to add a proxy or leave the gap honest.
